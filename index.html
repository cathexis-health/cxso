Cathexis Somatic Ontology (CSO), release 2.0.0

An open vocabulary for first-person somatic experience. Ontology IRI https://w3id.org/cxso. Files: Turtle, RDF/XML, JSON-LD context, identifier table, data rules. License CC BY 4.0. Cathexis Health LLC.

CodeLabelNotationDefinitionSynonyms
CXSO:0000001Somatic EntityThe root class for every entity in the Cathexis Somatic Ontology.
CXSO:0000002Somatic Data PointA single sensation report: where in the body (zone), what kind (quality), and optionally how intense (a whole number from 0 to 10). The atomic unit of somatic data.
CXSO:0000003Somatic SnapshotA complete body state at a single moment. It holds zero or more data points; with none, nothing stands out may record that the person attended to their sensations and found nothing standing out.
CXSO:0000004Somatic TrajectoryA body state over time: an ordered series of snapshots for one person. Aggregates computed from the series are an implementation concern, not part of the ontology.
CXSO:0000005Blanket ComponentOne of the four components of Friston's Markov blanket formalism (internal, external, sensory and active states). The clinical extension uses them to organize the classes that describe a person.
CXSO:0000006Internal StateThe person's physiological and psychological condition as experienced through interoception. What the person builds observer capacity for.
CXSO:0000007External StateEnvironmental conditions influencing internal experience. What the person cannot directly control but must relate to.
CXSO:0000008Sensory StateThe boundary where external becomes internal: the translation mechanism. Where prediction lives. The most clinically important component.
CXSO:0000009Active StateThe person's repertoire of responses: actions to minimize prediction error. The intervention library as a toolkit of active states.
CXSO:0000010Body ZoneAn area of the body where a person reports a sensation. A zone is the area a person reports, not a precise anatomical boundary; it is linked to UBERON where a corresponding structure exists.

CXSO:0000040Sensation QualityA phenomenological sensation descriptor. Somatic qualities, not emotional labels. Defined phenomenologically (what it feels like) rather than mechanistically (what causes it).
CXSO:0000041TightnessConstriction, gripping, clenching. FEAR activation, muscular bracing, suppressed expression. Most commonly reported quality across populations.clenching, constriction, gripping, knot, stiffness, tense, tension, tight

CXSO:0000059FloatingUntethered, drifting sensation. Living in an Unfamiliar Life: groundlessness. Living Under Siege: dissociative lightness. Distinct from Lightness, which has buoyancy: floating has no anchor.drifting, spacey, unanchored
CXSO:0000060TremblingFine involuntary shaking, felt or seen as movement. Somatic discharge: can be regulatory (release after activation) or a sign of distress (Living Under Siege freeze-thaw, Living in Overwhelm spillover).quivering, shaking, shivering
CXSO:0000061Territory of Human ExperienceA distinct pattern of neurobiological organization. Not a diagnosis; describes how a person's nervous system is currently organized and what that organization feels like from the inside. In Pessoa's terms, a characteristic pattern of dynamic functional coalition formation.
CXSO:0000062Living in ThreatT1High-precision threat predictions; ambiguous stimuli interpreted as dangerous.
CXSO:0000063Living in ShutdownT2Collapsed generative model; predictions of low return suppress approach behavior.
CXSO:0000064Living in LossT3The model of the world with the person in it persists after the person is gone; prediction errors on absence.
CXSO:0000065Living in DisconnectionT4Relational predictions suppressed; intimacy modeled as dangerous.
CXSO:0000066Living in OverwhelmT5Multiple high-precision predictions competing simultaneously; regulatory capacity exceeded.
CXSO:0000067Living Under SiegeT6Locked threat predictions with extreme precision; inability to update despite safety.
CXSO:0000068Living in Inner ConflictT7Contradictory predictions with high precision in opposing directions.
CXSO:0000069Living in the Body Under StrainT8Brain's pain predictions maintained despite changed peripheral input; pain as construct.
CXSO:0000070Living Under Pressure From OutsideT9Accurate predictions of genuine systemic threat; the stress response is appropriate.
CXSO:0000071Living in an Unfamiliar LifeT10Generative model of self under active reconstruction; predictions temporarily unreliable.
CXSO:0000072Living in ShameT11Self-model prediction of unworthiness; core identity prediction of defectiveness.
CXSO:0000073Living in Addiction and CompulsionT12SEEKING system hijacked by a substance or behavior; reward predictions distorted.
CXSO:0000074Living Behind a MaskT13False-self predictions maintained; authentic predictions suppressed.
CXSO:0000075Living in a World Built for Someone ElseT14Sensory processing differences; predictions calibrated to atypical neurology.
CXSO:0000076Living in Existential ConfrontationT15Confrontation with predictions that cannot be resolved: mortality, meaning, freedom.
CXSO:0000077Living With a Changing BrainT16Memory systems themselves degrading; predictions increasingly unreliable. Procedural memory is the most resilient system.
CXSO:0000078Blanket ConfigurationA characteristic pattern of Markov blanket organization shared by related territories.
CXSO:0000079Porosity patternA blanket organization in which sensory translations are overactive: the boundary is too thin.
CXSO:0000080Rigidity patternA blanket organization in which sensory signals are suppressed: the boundary is too thick.
CXSO:0000081Boundary patternA blanket organization in which the boundary is distorted: it is misshapen.
CXSO:0000082Damage patternA blanket organization in which the boundary is ruptured: it is torn.
CXSO:0000083Existential / Neurological patternA blanket organization in which predictions may be accurate, or cannot be tested.Existential / Neurological
CXSO:0000084Primary Affect SystemOne of Panksepp's seven genetically encoded primary affect systems with distinct neural circuitry.
CXSO:0000085SEEKINGDopaminergic motivation, exploration, curiosity. Suppression is a hallmark of Living in Shutdown.
CXSO:0000086FEARThreat detection and defensive behavior. The primary driver of Living in Threat. Locked activation of this system is central to Living Under Siege.
CXSO:0000087RAGEFrustration, assertive response. Suppressed RAGE appears as jaw tension.
CXSO:0000088PANIC/GRIEFSeparation distress, attachment. Primary system in Living in Loss. Suppressed in Living in Disconnection.
CXSO:0000089CARENurturing, bonding. Suppression indicates relational territory involvement.
CXSO:0000090PLAYSocial learning, joy, boundary-testing. Suppression is a sensitive clinical marker across all territories.
CXSO:0000091LUSTSexual motivation. Relevant to Living in Disconnection, Living Under Siege, and relational territories.

CXSO:0000112Brain NetworkA functional system of interacting brain regions, as described in the research the HES draws on. Networks are concepts of the theory, not anatomical structures, so the CSO defines them itself.
CXSO:0000113Default Mode NetworkSelf-referential processing, autobiographical memory, mind-wandering, future simulation. Active during rest. Overactive in rumination (Living in Shutdown).
CXSO:0000114Salience NetworkDetects what matters: threat, novelty, reward, social significance. The switchboard that routes attention between internal and external processing.
CXSO:0000115Central Executive NetworkCognitive control, working memory, goal-directed behavior, strategic planning.
CXSO:0000116Interoceptive NetworkBody signal processing. Where the body's internal state becomes conscious felt experience.
CXSO:0000117Threat / Fear CircuitRapid threat detection and defensive response. Panksepp's FEAR system plus cortical monitoring.
CXSO:0000118Reward / Motivation NetworkPanksepp's SEEKING system. Dopaminergic circuits that drive exploration, engagement, and approach behavior.
CXSO:0000119Social Brain NetworkSocial cognition: reading faces, voices, intentions, and relational context. Models other minds and tracks social dynamics.
CXSO:0000120Memory Consolidation NetworkConsolidates experience into durable learning and contextualizes the present with the past. Active during reconsolidation and model updating.
CXSO:0000121Attachment / Loss NetworkPanksepp's PANIC/GRIEF circuitry. Processes separation distress, proximity-seeking, and the somatic weight of loss (Living in Loss, Living in Disconnection).
CXSO:0000122PLAY NetworkPanksepp's PLAY system. Social joy, spontaneity, and boundary-testing. Suppression is a cross-territory marker of chronic strain; its return marks recovery.
CXSO:0000123Somatomotor NetworkMotor execution, body schema, proprioception. The action network, engaged by movement-based regulation practices.
CXSO:0000124Bronfenbrenner LayerAn ecological systems layer from Bronfenbrenner's developmental ecology. Used to classify external state entities by their proximity and influence type.
CXSO:0000125MicrosystemImmediate relationships and settings: partner, family, close friends, workplace team, home.
CXSO:0000126MesosystemInteractions between microsystems: work-family conflict, school-home dynamics.
CXSO:0000127ExosystemIndirect environments that affect the individual: partner's workplace stress, community resources, media.
CXSO:0000128MacrosystemBroad cultural and societal patterns: economic conditions, political climate, cultural norms.
CXSO:0000129ChronosystemTransitions and shifts over time: life transitions, historical events, developmental changes.
CXSO:0000130Change MechanismA therapeutic change mechanism, each engaging a distinct memory system.
CXSO:0000131Memory ReconsolidationOpens and updates emotional memory during the reconsolidation window (approximately 4 to 6 hours).
CXSO:0000132New Habit FormationBuilds new procedural patterns through repetition and reward.
CXSO:0000133Model UpdatingUpdates explicit beliefs and self-narratives through new information and reflection.
CXSO:0000134Learning LevelOne of six capacities a person builds. They tend to develop in this order, and each earlier capacity supports the ones after it, but people move between levels and may work on several at once.
